Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the BMI calculator?

BMI is a useful screening tool but has limitations. It does not distinguish between muscle and fat, so athletes may have a high BMI despite being healthy. For a more complete picture, use our body fat estimator alongside BMI.

How does the Navy method body fat calculator work?

The Navy method estimates body fat percentage using neck and waist circumference measurements along with height. It's based on formulas developed by the US Navy and provides a reasonable estimate for most people, though it may not be as accurate as DEXA scans or hydrostatic weighing.

Is my data saved on your servers?

No. All data you enter is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal health data to our servers. Your data stays on your device.

Can I export my tracking history?

Yes. Go to the Export tab in the calculator to download your data as JSON or CSV. This allows you to back up your records or import them into other applications.

Should I rely on BMI alone for health assessment?

No. BMI is just one metric and does not tell the whole story. We recommend tracking BMI alongside body fat percentage, waist circumference, and overall fitness levels. Always consult a healthcare provider for personalized health advice.
