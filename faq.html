Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely free. No account, no signup, no credit card. I built this because I needed it and figured others might too.

Is my data saved?

Yes, but only on your device. We use your browser's localStorage, which means your history stays on your computer/phone. Nothing is sent to our servers. You can clear it anytime by clearing your browser data.

How accurate is the body fat calculator?

We use the U.S. Navy method, which studies show is accurate within 3-4% compared to DEXA scans (the gold standard). For best results, measure your waist and neck at the same spots consistently. I measure my waist at the navel level, neck below the Adam's apple.

Why did my BMI go up but I look better?

This is exactly what happened to me. Muscle weighs more than fat. When you build muscle and lose fat, your weight (and therefore BMI) can increase while your body composition improves. That's why we track body fat percentage alongside BMI.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes, the site is fully responsive. I actually use it on my phone every Sunday morning to check my weekly trend.

Do you give medical advice?

No. This tool is for informational purposes only. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, especially if you have existing health conditions.

How do I export my data?

Go to the Export tab in the calculator. You can download your history as JSON or CSV. The JSON format is useful if you want to import into another app. CSV opens in Excel.
