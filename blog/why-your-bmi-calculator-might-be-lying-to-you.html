Why Your BMI Calculator Might Be Lying to You

I used to trust those online BMI calculators blindly. Type in height, type in weight, get a number, done.

But after 15 years in clinical practice, I can tell you: it is not that simple.



The standard BMI formula does not account for muscle mass, bone density, or even ethnic differences. I have seen athletes classified as "overweight" when they are in peak condition.





Look, I am not saying BMI is useless. It is a screening tool, not a diagnostic. But if you are making health decisions based on a single number from a basic calculator, you are doing it wrong.
