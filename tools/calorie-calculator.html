How It Works

We calculate your BMR first, then multiply by your activity factor to get TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure).

Activity Multipliers:

  • Sedentary: 1.2
  • Light exercise: 1.375
  • Moderate exercise: 1.55
  • Active: 1.725
  • Very active: 1.9

Then we adjust by your goal: -500 calories per day ~ 1 lb fat loss per week. +500 ~ 1 lb gain per week.
