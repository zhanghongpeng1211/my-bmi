How It Works

WHtR = waist circumference / height. It's that simple.

  • Below 0.5: Low risk. Your waist is less than half your height.
  • 0.5 to 0.6: Moderate risk. Time to pay attention.
  • Above 0.6: High risk. Consult a healthcare provider.

Research suggests WHtR is a better predictor of cardiovascular risk than BMI because it accounts for abdominal fat — the dangerous kind.

Measure waist at the level of your navel, after exhaling. Don't suck in.
