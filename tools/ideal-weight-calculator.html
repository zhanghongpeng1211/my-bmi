How It Works

Different formulas were developed for different populations and purposes:

  • Devine (1974): Originally for medication dosing. Most commonly cited in medicine.
  • Robinson (1983): Updated Devine formula with more recent data.
  • Miller (1983): Based on Metropolitan Life Insurance tables.
  • Hamwi (1964): Simple rule of thumb. 48kg for 5ft men + 2.7kg per inch. 45.5kg for 5ft women + 2.2kg per inch.

All of these ignore body composition. A muscular person can be "overweight" by every formula and still be healthy.
