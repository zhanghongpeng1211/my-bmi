How It Works

BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)². It's a screening tool used by healthcare providers to identify weight categories.

  • Underweight: BMI below 18.5
  • Normal: BMI 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25 to 29.9
  • Obese: BMI 30 or higher

Note: BMI doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or body composition. Athletes may have a high BMI without excess fat.
