Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Our Commitment

Your health data is personal. We don't want it, we don't collect it, and we can't sell what we don't have.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not collect or store your BMI, weight, or body fat data on our servers
  • We do not require you to create an account
  • We do not track your personal identity
  • We do not sell data to third parties
  • We do not use your health data for advertising

What We Do

We use your browser's localStorage to save your calculation history. This means:

  • Your data stays on your device only
  • We cannot access your data
  • You can delete it anytime by clearing browser data
  • Exporting data creates a file on your device, not on our servers

Analytics

We use basic analytics (like Google Analytics) to understand how many people visit the site. This is anonymous and does not include your health data.

Advertising

We may display ads to support the site. These ads are not personalized based on your health data because we don't have it.

Contact

If you have privacy concerns, email: privacy@my-bmi.net
