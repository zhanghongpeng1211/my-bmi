Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

The Short Version

We don't collect your data. We don't have a server. We don't use cookies. We don't track you. Your BMI calculations and weight history are stored in your browser's localStorage and never leave your computer.

What Data We Collect

None. Zero. Nada. This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. There is no backend, no database, no analytics tracking, no user accounts.

How Your Data Is Stored

Any data you enter (height, weight, BMI calculations) is stored in your browser's localStorage. This means:

  • It stays on your device only
  • We can't access it
  • No one else can access it
  • It persists until you clear your browser data

Third Parties

We don't share data with third parties because we don't have any data to share. We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any tracking scripts.
