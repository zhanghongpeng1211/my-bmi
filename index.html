Personalized Advice

Track Consistently

Weigh yourself at the same time daily (morning, after bathroom, before breakfast). I tracked for 365 days and the trend data changed everything.

Measure Body Fat, Not Just Weight

BMI alone fooled me for months. I was "skinny fat" — normal BMI but 22% body fat. Use the Navy method above; it's free and surprisingly accurate.

Focus on Protein

When I increased protein to 150g/day, my body fat dropped from 22% to 16% in 3 months while my BMI actually went up. Muscle weighs more than fat.
